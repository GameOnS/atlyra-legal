# Privacy policy for Atlyra

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This privacy policy covers the Atlyra app for Android phones and Wear OS watches as well as this website.

## In short

- Atlyra has no user account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics or crash reporting of its own. Two Google libraries in the app do send diagnostic data to Google, though: the one for purchases through Google Play every time the app starts, and the barcode scanner every time it is opened (see "Atlyra Pro and purchases" and "Barcode scanner").
- Atlyra initially stores your training and nutrition data on your device. We as the provider do not see it – with one exception: if you use the assistant Atlyra AI and consent to it, the data needed for your question goes through our server to an AI provider (see "Atlyra AI").
- Data may also leave your device for optional backups to your Google Drive, the connection to your watch, Android backup, or when you export or share something. Purchases and emails use Google Play or your email app. When you search online for a product or look up a barcode in the nutrition diary, the request goes to the food database Open Food Facts (see "Nutrition diary").
- The watch app only measures your heart rate if you agree to it on the watch. The readings stay on your devices (see "Heart rate measurement with the watch").
- Health Connect is an optional connection: only once you set it up does Atlyra read health data from other apps – such as sleep, steps or weight – and store summaries of it on your device. It does not go to Atlyra AI (see "Health Connect").

## What the app stores

Atlyra initially stores your entries locally in the app's protected storage. The backups described below may optionally create copies:

- Workouts: exercises, sets, weights, reps, duration, distance, effort (RPE), notes and times
- Routines and custom exercises
- Body measurements if you enter them: body weight, body fat percentage and circumferences
- Heart rate and calories if you enter them manually for cardio exercises
- Heart rate during a workout and an estimate of the energy burned, if the watch app measures them with your agreement (see "Heart rate measurement with the watch")
- Points, ranks and levels that the app calculates from your workouts
- Settings such as units, weekly goal and rest time
- Result of the age check: on first start the app asks for your date of birth, because some features have a minimum age: the app cannot be used under the age of 13, the backup to Google Drive and Health Connect are open from 16, and Atlyra AI and the suggested calorie goal from 18. The date is checked on your device and then neither kept nor transmitted. Only the result is stored: for adults, that they are of age; for people under 18, the day of their 18th birthday, so that the features open by themselves – the date of birth can be read from that day. It is part of the settings, is not included in the Drive backup, and stays stored until you clear the app's data in the Android settings or uninstall the app.
- Coaching profile if you fill it in: training goal, experience, preferred session length, equipment, focus areas, exercises you would rather not do, and a note. It stays on the device and is included neither in the Drive backup nor in Android's backup.
- Nutrition diary if you use it: entries with food, amount, meal, day and nutrition values, your own foods, saved meals and recipes, and your nutrition goals, along with the water you log and your supplements with their times and the intakes you tick off. For calculating a calorie goal you can give your year of birth, height, sex and activity; this is not required.
- Values from Health Connect if you connect it: processed daily values of the last 90 days, such as sleep duration, steps, weight and nutrition (see "Health Connect"). They are not part of any backup.

Body measurements, heart rate, the nutrition diary, values from Health Connect and the note in the coaching profile (for example about an injury) can be health data. Apart from the age check, all entries are optional. Only the watch app reads sensors, and only if you agree there to having your heart rate measured (see "Heart rate measurement with the watch"). The app only accesses Health Connect if you connect it (see "Health Connect").

The only purpose is to provide the app's features: recording workouts, showing history and statistics, managing routines, recording and evaluating nutrition.

**Retention and deletion:** Local data is kept until you delete it. Settings → "Backup & data" → "Delete all workout data" removes workouts including the heart rate measured during them, routines and body measurements from your phone, along with the chats with Atlyra AI; custom exercises remain. The nutrition diary is deleted separately (see "Nutrition diary"), the values from Health Connect with "Disconnect" (see "Health Connect"). This does not delete Google Drive or Android backups or data on your watch. When you uninstall the app, Android removes local app data, but existing backups may remain. The steps for deleting Drive backups are below.

## Connection to your Wear OS watch

If you use the watch app, the phone and the watch exchange workouts, routines, exercise names, the current workout as well as points and ranks – and the heart rate, if the watch measures it. The transfer uses the wearable interface of Google Play services: directly via Bluetooth or, if that is not available, via Google servers. According to Google, this transfer is end-to-end encrypted and only Atlyra on your own paired devices can read the data. Workouts and heart rate measurements stored on the watch stay there until they have been synced with the phone.

## Heart rate measurement with the watch (optional)

The watch app can measure your heart rate during a workout. This only happens when three things come together: the feature is switched on in the phone app (Settings → "Watch" → "Measure heart rate with the watch"), you answer the watch's question "Measure heart rate?" with "Measure", and you allow the watch app to use the sensor when the system asks.

- **What is measured:** your heart rate from the watch's sensor, summarised as one value per five seconds, from the start of the measurement to the end of the workout. If you also allow the watch app to access "physical activity", an estimate of the energy burned (calories) is added, which the watch's system calculates. Atlyra measures nothing outside a workout.
- **What for:** to show you the current heart rate during the workout and, afterwards, the average, the maximum and the estimated calories.
- **Where the values are kept:** on the watch during the workout, then with the workout on your phone. They are transferred as described in the section "Connection to your Wear OS watch". They are part of your backups (file, Google Drive, Android backup) as far as you use these. We as the provider never see them, and they are not sent to Atlyra AI.
- **Switching off and deleting:** You stop the measurement on the watch by tapping the heart rate on the first page of the workout, or permanently in the phone app's settings; you withdraw the permission in the watch's settings. Measured values go when you delete the workout. If a workout is discarded, Atlyra discards its measurement as well.
- **Limits:** A sensor on the wrist is less accurate during strength training than a chest strap, and the calories are an estimate. The values are not a medical measurement.

## Health Connect (optional)

Under Settings → "Health & data sources" you can connect Atlyra to Health Connect, the Android interface through which apps such as Samsung Health or MyFitnessPal make their data available. Without this connection Atlyra reads no data from other apps, and all other features work as before.

- **What Atlyra reads:** only the areas you select and allow in Health Connect – sleep (duration and sleep stages), steps, workouts from other apps (type, start, end, name), body weight and nutrition (energy, protein, carbohydrates, fat per entry). In addition, the name of the app a value comes from. Atlyra requests no other data types, in particular no medical records.
- **Why:** so Atlyra can look at your training together with sleep, everyday activity, weight and nutrition – in notes on the home screen and in the weight history.
- **Where the data is kept:** Atlyra stores processed values of the last 90 days (for example sleep duration per night, steps per day) in the app's protected storage on your device. They are excluded from Android backup and are part of neither the backup file nor the Google Drive backup. We as the provider never see them.
- **No sharing with Atlyra AI:** the assistant receives neither data from Health Connect nor summaries of it; there is no setting to share them.
- **Transferring workouts:** if you switch it on and allow it in Health Connect, Atlyra writes finished workouts to Health Connect as a session: name, start, end and type. Sets, weights and reps are not transferred. Other apps you give access to in Health Connect can read these sessions. If you change or delete a workout in Atlyra, the app changes or deletes the session in Health Connect as well.
- **Sending the nutrition diary:** if you switch it on and allow it in Health Connect, Atlyra writes the entries of your nutrition diary to Health Connect. For each entry that is the name of the food (with the brand, if given), the meal, a point in time and the calories and nutrition values of the amount you logged, as far as known: protein, carbohydrates, sugar, fat, saturated fat, fibre and sodium. Entries from the day you switch it on are sent, earlier ones are not. Other apps you give access to nutrition data in Health Connect can read these entries – including the names of the foods. If you change or delete an entry in Atlyra, the app changes or deletes it in Health Connect as well. Atlyra does not read back what it has sent itself.
- **No sharing with third parties:** Atlyra does not sell data from Health Connect, does not use it for advertising and passes it to no one.
- **Legal basis:** your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give by connecting and by granting the permissions in Health Connect.
- **Withdrawing and deleting:** you switch individual areas off on the "Health & data sources" page; Atlyra then deletes the values stored for them. When you switch off the transfer of workouts or of the nutrition diary, the app asks whether what has been sent so far should stay in Health Connect or be removed there. "Disconnect" stops reading and sending, gives the permissions back and deletes all cached values. You can also revoke permissions in Health Connect itself at any time. What is stored in Health Connect and in the other apps is not affected by disconnecting – including workouts and diary entries Atlyra has sent there. If you do not want to keep them there, switch the transfer off with "Remove there" first, or delete them in Health Connect.
- **Limits:** values from watches, scales and nutrition apps are estimates and self-reported entries. Atlyra draws no diagnoses from them and is no substitute for seeing a doctor.

## Backup to your Google Drive (optional)

If you explicitly consent to connecting Google Drive in the settings, you sign in with Google through Google Play services and allow Atlyra to access a hidden app folder. You cannot view individual backup files in the regular Drive interface; Atlyra can read them for backup and restore. The app has no access to your other files in Google Drive.

- **What is transferred:** a compressed copy of all training data including body measurements and heart rate (entered manually or measured by the watch), and – if you use it – of the nutrition diary (entries, your own foods, saved meals and recipes, goals), plus the number of workouts as extra information on the backup file. Atlyra does not additionally encrypt this copy; transfer to Google Drive uses HTTPS. The ten newest backups are kept; the app deletes older ones.
- **When:** when you tap "Back up now" and – with Atlyra Pro – automatically every day and shortly after each workout.
- **Stored on the device:** the email address of the connected Google account (for display), time and checksum of the last backup.
- **Who receives the data:** The backups are stored in your own Google account. We have no access to them. Google's processing is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). For users in Europe the provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Data may also be transferred to the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
- **Legal basis:** your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give by tapping "Consent and connect". You can withdraw it at any time with "Disconnect": Atlyra stops further backups and removes the locally stored connection. Backups already uploaded remain unless you delete them (see the next item).
- **Deleting backups:** In the app under Settings → "Backup & data" → "Delete backups in Google Drive": Atlyra then deletes all of its backups from your Google Drive, and the connection stays. Or when disconnecting: "Disconnect" and tick "Also delete the backups in Google Drive" there – Atlyra deletes them before it disconnects. At any time without the app: in Google Drive in your browser, go to Settings → Manage apps → Atlyra → Options → "Delete hidden app data".

## Nutrition diary (optional)

In the nutrition diary you record what you eat and, if you like, set goals for calories and nutrients. Whether you use it is up to you; training works without it. Like your training data, the diary is stored on your device. We do not see it.

- **Food database in the app:** The search for ordinary foods uses an extract of the German food composition database (Bundeslebensmittelschlüssel) that is installed with the app. Nothing is transmitted.
- **Product search and barcode (Open Food Facts):** When you search online for a packaged product or look up a barcode, the app queries the open food database Open Food Facts (Association Open Food Facts, 21 rue des Iles, 94100 Saint-Maur des Fossés, France). Transmitted are your search term or the barcode, the language of the search, and the app's name and version with our contact address – the service requires that from apps. As with any request on the internet, your IP address is processed. Your diary, your body measurements and your goals are not transmitted; there is no account. Before the first search the app points this out, and without your confirmation it sends nothing. A product you log is stored with its nutrition values in your own list so that it is available offline afterwards. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: the transmission is necessary to carry out the search you start. Processing by Open Food Facts is governed by their [privacy policy](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy).
- **Barcode scanner:** The scanner is provided by Google Play services (Google's ML Kit). They show the camera image and return only the number that was read to Atlyra; Atlyra itself gets neither access to the camera nor an image. Every time the scanner is opened, ML Kit creates a diagnostic entry in the app and sends it to Google, encrypted, in batches and with a delay, often only the next day: device model, manufacturer, Android version and build, the device's language, country and time zone, the identifier of the mobile network, Atlyra's package name and version, a random identifier that ML Kit creates for this installation, and how long the scanner was open and how it was closed; with automatic zoom also the zoom levels and the approximate position of the code in the image. The code that was read and images are not part of it. Google states that it uses this data to diagnose the service and analyse its usage and does not pass it on to third parties. Atlyra receives and stores none of it. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in offering a reliable scanner for which Atlyra itself needs no access to the camera (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Processing by Google is governed by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Google's [ML Kit data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure). You can type the number instead.
- **Goals and goal check:** The app estimates a calorie goal on the device from the details you give. The goal check works out – also on the device – from four weeks of diary and your body weight whether the goal fits what you burn. Both are estimates and not dietary advice.
- **Water and supplements:** The water you log and the supplements you add (name, amount, times, days of the week), together with the intakes you tick off, are stored only on your device. The app schedules the reminders as alarms on the device; nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Atlyra AI and Health Connect do not receive this information.
- **Backups:** The diary, including water and supplements, is included in the Google Drive backup, in the exported backup and in Android's backup if you use them (see there).
- **Deleting:** In the nutrition diary, the menu → "Delete all nutrition data" removes the diary, water and supplements, your own foods, saved meals and recipes, goals and the details for the goal calculation from this device. "Delete all workout data" in the settings leaves the nutrition diary in place.
- **Health Connect:** If you switch it on, Atlyra sends the diary's entries to Health Connect, where other apps can read them with your permission. The other way round, you can have the daily values of another nutrition app shown from Health Connect instead of your own diary (see "Health Connect").
- **Atlyra AI:** The assistant does not see the nutrition diary. It only looks at a photo of a meal if you send one yourself (see "Atlyra AI").

## Atlyra AI (optional)

Atlyra AI is an assistant inside the app. It answers questions about your training and proposes changes to your routines and, if you ask, swapping an exercise in the workout you are doing. It does not receive body measurements, the nutrition diary or data from Health Connect. If you take a photo of a meal in the nutrition diary, it looks at it on request and proposes the entries. Unlike the rest of the app, it does not work on your device alone: to answer, the app sends data to a server.

Atlyra AI only becomes active once you explicitly consent when you first open it. Without consent nothing is transmitted, and every other feature of the app stays available as before. Atlyra AI is open only to adults aged 18 and over – this is why the app asks for your date of birth on first start – and is currently only available in the European Economic Area and Switzerland. The exception is Google Play's reviewers in review mode: for them, Anthropic's list of countries applies.

- **What is transmitted:** your messages to the assistant, the conversation so far and the training data needed for the question at hand. This can include routines, exercise names, notes, workouts with sets, weights and reps, and figures computed from them. This includes your coaching profile as far as you have filled it in (goal, experience, preferred session length, equipment, focus areas, exercises you would rather not do, and your note); you can switch this sharing off separately under "Privacy & data". Body weight and body measurements, the nutrition diary and data from Health Connect are not transmitted: Atlyra AI has no setting to share them, and the assistant cannot fetch them. A photo of a meal is only included if you take it yourself in the nutrition diary and have consented to that separately beforehand; no images can be sent in the conversation with the coach. The assistant then receives only the picture and may look things up in the app's food database – it does not see your diary, goals, saved meals or weight. The app scales the picture down and saves it anew, so details from the file such as location and camera are dropped; it is transmitted with the one request it belongs to. After that neither the app nor our server keeps the image: the shot sits in the app's cache only briefly and is deleted as soon as it has been read. At Anthropic the same retention applies as for the other inputs. A picture often shows more than the food: make sure no people, documents or screens are in it. You withdraw the consent for photos under "Privacy & data" with the "Meal photos" switch. Before anything leaves your device, the app replaces email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses, links, IBANs, card numbers and IP addresses with a placeholder – in your messages, in notes and other free text the assistant reads, and in reported answers. The filter recognises patterns, not everything: it does not replace names or details about your health. So what you write into a message yourself largely goes along – only write there what it should know for your question. The app never transmits your entire data set. In addition there is technical information: a checksum of the identifier Android assigns to this app on your device (Android ID), the app's language and units, the app version, the offset of your device's time zone from UTC – so that daily counts start again at your midnight –, a proof of authenticity from Google Play and – if you have Atlyra Pro – the purchase identifier from Google Play. If the review mode for Google Play's reviewers is unlocked, a checksum is added that shows the reviewer password was entered; the password itself is not transmitted. As with any internet connection, Cloudflare receives your IP address and derives the country and region from it; our server only gets that result, to check whether Atlyra AI may be offered there, and does not read the address itself.
- **Who receives the data:** The request goes to a server operated by us at Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). It checks the request and passes it on to Anthropic (Anthropic Ireland, Limited, 6th Floor, South Bank House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, D04 TR29, Ireland), whose language model Claude generates the answer. Both process the data on our behalf. To verify the authenticity of the app and a Pro purchase, the server queries Google; for users in Europe the provider is Google Ireland Limited. Data is also transferred to the USA. Cloudflare, Inc. and Google LLC are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; with Anthropic the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission apply. They are part of Anthropic's [Data Processing Addendum](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/data-processing-addendum); we will send you a copy on request.
- **What is stored:** Our server stores no conversations – with one exception that you trigger yourself: answers you report (see "Reporting an answer"). It stores usage counters, linked to a non-reversible checksum of the purchase identifier or – without Pro – of the device identifier. It forms the checksum of the device identifier with a secret only it knows, so nobody can work out from an Android ID which counter belongs to it. With Atlyra Pro it deletes them 40 days after your last request. Without Pro, Atlyra AI is only available as a trial with a fixed number of requests per device; so that this trial does not start over, its counter is kept until your device has not contacted Atlyra AI for three years. The device identifier stays the same when you clear the app's data or reinstall it; it only changes when the device is reset. If you have Atlyra Pro, it also keeps, under the checksum of the purchase identifier, the time until which your purchase is paid according to Google Play – so that you keep Pro if Google does not answer the check once. This note is deleted one day after that time, or as soon as Google no longer confirms the purchase. Our server neither reads nor stores your IP address. For each request our server logs only figures: tier, duration, size, cost and an error code – no conversation content and no identifiers. Cloudflare keeps these logs, together with technical details of the call, for up to seven days and processes your IP address to deliver the request and to protect its network. According to Anthropic, it deletes inputs and outputs within 30 days and does not use them to train its models. Anthropic keeps content longer – up to two years – only if its automated abuse detection flags it as a violation of its usage policy, or if a law requires it. Your chats themselves are stored only on your device and are excluded from Android backup. Each one is deleted after 30 days without use, and at most the 50 most recently used are kept.
- **Voice input:** In the chat with the coach you can dictate your question. When you tap the microphone, your device's speech recognition opens (usually Google's); it listens and turns what you say into text. Atlyra records nothing and has no access to the microphone: the app only receives the recognised text in the input field, and only when you send it does it go to Atlyra AI like a typed question. What happens to your voice is governed by the provider of the speech recognition in its own privacy policy.
- **Changes to your data:** Atlyra AI saves nothing on its own. Proposals appear as a preview and are only applied once you confirm them.
- **Reporting an answer:** With "Report answer" you can send us an answer from the assistant that is wrong, dangerous or offensive. What is transmitted is the text of that answer, the reason you choose, your note, the language and the app version – not the conversation and no identifier. If the answer mentions training, nutrition, health or body data, that is included; the dialog says so before you send it. We keep the report for 30 days on our server at Cloudflare and use it only to recognise unsuitable answers and to improve the assistant. Because it carries no identifier, we cannot link it to you afterwards. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by sending it (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR).
- **Legal basis:** your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time in Atlyra AI under "Privacy & data". Withdrawing deletes your chats on your device and stops further transmissions. The checks against abuse (counters, proof of authenticity, country) are based on our legitimate interest in secure operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Limits:** Answers from an AI can be wrong. Atlyra AI does not give medical advice and is no substitute for seeing a doctor.

The Google Play library for purchases that the app contains for this purpose sends diagnostic data to Google, encrypted, every time the app starts – even without a purchase and even when the app only starts in the background, for example for a reminder: device model, manufacturer, Android version and build, language, country, time zone and the identifier of the mobile network, the package name and version of Atlyra and of the library, a session identifier newly created at each start, and the result and duration of the connection to Google Play. Atlyra receives none of it. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in offering Atlyra Pro through Google Play, as Google requires for purchases in apps (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

For billing, refunds and tax purposes, Google provides us with order information in the Google Play Console, such as order number, product, price, date and country. We only use it to process your purchase (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and to meet statutory retention obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR), which can be up to ten years. Google's processing is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The purchase is subject to the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/).

## Sharing

When you share a workout as text or as an image card (rank, records, level-up, achievements), the app creates the text or image on your device and hands it to the app you choose in Android's share dialog. Atlyra itself sends nothing to us or to third parties. What the chosen app does with the content is governed by that app's privacy terms. Workout routines are shared as a link. The routine – name, exercises, sets, reps, rest times and notes, but no weights – is contained in the link itself. No server stores it. ## Home screen widgets (optional)

Atlyra offers widgets for your home screen: for your next or current workout, this week's workouts, a workout routine, a personal best and – if you use the nutrition diary – the calories and protein logged today. When you place one, Atlyra hands the displayed content to your home screen app on your device, which shows it. Nothing is transmitted over the internet for this, neither to us nor to third parties. What the home screen app does with the content is governed by that app's privacy terms. Anyone who looks at your unlocked home screen can see the content. Which routine, exercise and colors a widget shows is stored by Atlyra on your device; when you remove the widget, that setting is deleted.

## Android backup

Atlyra allows Android's built-in backup. If it is enabled in your device settings, Android can back up the app's data – including the nutrition diary – to your Google account and restore it on a new device. This backup is controlled by Android and Google. We have no access to it. You can turn it off in Android's settings under "Google" → "Backup". The values read from Health Connect and the settings for it are excluded from this backup.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** for the rest timer and the current workout. The app only asks when you start a workout.
- **Vibration:** for the rest timer.
- **Alarms and keeping the watch awake (watch app):** so the watch can signal the end of a rest with a vibration on time, even while its screen is off. Nothing is transferred for this.
- **Body sensors and physical activity (watch app):** for measuring the heart rate during a workout and for the calorie estimate that goes with it. The watch only asks for them once you have agreed to the measurement. On watches with Wear OS 6 or later the first permission is called "heart rate". Without "physical activity" the watch measures the heart rate only.
- **Foreground service for health data (watch app):** so the heart rate measurement continues while the watch's screen is off. It only runs during a workout in which the heart rate is measured.
- **Internet and network state:** for Google Drive backups, purchases through Google Play, Atlyra AI and the product search at Open Food Facts.
- **Background execution** (wake lock, foreground service, start after reboot): so that automatic backups run reliably in the background and home screen widgets get updated.
- **Google Play billing:** for buying Atlyra Pro.
- **Health Connect:** reading sleep, steps, workouts, weight and nutrition, and writing workouts and entries of the nutrition diary – each only once you switch the feature on and allow it in Health Connect.

Atlyra does not request permissions for location, contacts, camera or microphone. Only the watch app uses the body sensors, and only for measuring the heart rate. The barcode scanner in the nutrition diary comes from Google Play services and needs no camera permission from Atlyra. You take a photo for Atlyra AI with your device's camera app; Atlyra receives only that one shot and needs neither a camera permission nor access to your photos for it. Likewise, for voice input in the coach your device's speech recognition does the listening; Atlyra receives only the recognised text and needs no microphone permission.

## Data security

The data is stored in the app's private storage. Connections to Google Drive, to the Atlyra AI server and to Open Food Facts, as well as the diagnostic data of the Google libraries, are encrypted via HTTPS, and the data exchange with the watch is encrypted. There is no database at the provider where users' training data comes together; for Atlyra AI our server stores only what is described there: usage counters, for Atlyra Pro the paid period, and answers you report yourself.

## Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 15 to 21 and Art. 7(3) GDPR).

Because we have no access to your training data, you can view, change, export or delete local data directly in the app. You delete single chats with Atlyra AI there in the list of chats, and all of them at once under "Privacy & data". Values cached from Health Connect are deleted under "Health & data sources" with "Disconnect". What Anthropic keeps until it deletes it after 30 days cannot be linked to a person by us: there is no account, and no identifier of yours is sent to Anthropic (Art. 11 GDPR). You need to manage Drive and Android backups separately; deleting data on your phone does not remove them. For anything else, email us at [herzblut.studio@web.de](mailto:herzblut.studio@web.de).

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one where you live.
