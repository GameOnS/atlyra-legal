# Privacy policy for Satzwerk

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This privacy policy covers the Satzwerk app for Android phones and Wear OS watches as well as this website.

## In short

- Satzwerk has no user account, no server of its own, no ads, no tracking and no analytics or crash reporting.
- Your training data stays on your device. We as the provider never see it.
- Data only leaves your device when you want it to: for backups to your own Google Drive, for the connection to your watch, when you buy Satzwerk Pro through Google Play, and when you export or share something yourself or send us an email.

## What the app stores

Satzwerk stores everything you enter only locally on your device, in the app's protected storage that other apps cannot access:

- Workouts: exercises, sets, weights, reps, duration, distance, effort (RPE), notes and times
- Routines and custom exercises
- Body measurements if you enter them: body weight, body fat percentage and circumferences
- Heart rate and calories if you enter them manually for cardio exercises
- Points, ranks and levels that the app calculates from your workouts
- Settings such as units, weekly goal and rest time

Body measurements and heart rate can be health data. All entries are optional. The app does not read any sensors and does not access Health Connect or other health apps.

The only purpose is to provide the app's features: recording workouts, showing history and statistics, managing routines.

**Retention:** The data is kept until you delete it. Settings → "Delete all training data" removes workouts, routines and body measurements. When you uninstall the app, Android deletes all locally stored data.

## Connection to your Wear OS watch

If you use the watch app, the phone and the watch exchange workouts, routines, exercise names, the current workout as well as points and ranks. The transfer uses the wearable interface of Google Play services: directly via Bluetooth or, if that is not available, via Google servers. According to Google, this transfer is end-to-end encrypted and only Satzwerk on your own paired devices can read the data. Workouts stored on the watch stay there until they have been synced with the phone.

## Backup to your Google Drive (optional)

If you choose "Connect to Google Drive" in the settings, you sign in with Google through Google Play services and allow access to a hidden folder reserved for Satzwerk. The app has no access to your other files in Google Drive.

- **What is transferred:** a compressed copy of all training data including body measurements, plus the number of workouts as extra information on the backup file. The ten newest backups are kept; the app deletes older ones.
- **When:** when you tap "Back up now" and – with Satzwerk Pro – automatically every day and shortly after each workout.
- **Stored on the device:** the email address of the connected Google account (for display), time and checksum of the last backup.
- **Who receives the data:** The backups are stored in your own Google account. We have no access to them. Google's processing is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). For users in Europe the provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Data may also be transferred to the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
- **Legal basis:** your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give by confirming the notice shown before connecting. You can withdraw it at any time with "Disconnect", which revokes the app's access to your Google Drive.
- **Deleting backups:** In Google Drive in your browser, go to Settings → Manage apps → Satzwerk → Options → "Delete hidden app data".

## Satzwerk Pro and purchases

Purchases of Satzwerk Pro are handled by Google Play. You only enter your payment details with Google; we never receive them. The app only receives purchase information from Google Play (product, status and a purchase identifier) to unlock Pro, and remembers locally whether Pro is active. On start, the app also checks once, locally, whether training data already exists so that early users keep their existing features. Nothing is transferred for this.

For billing, refunds and tax purposes, Google provides us with order information in the Google Play Console, such as order number, product, price, date and country. We only use it to process your purchase (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and to meet statutory retention obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR), which can be up to ten years. Google's processing is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The purchase is subject to the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/).

## Rating on Google Play

After a good workout, the app may ask Google Play to show you a rating window – at most twice. Google Play decides whether and when it appears. The app does not learn whether you rate or what you write. You submit your rating directly to Google. It appears in the Play Store under your Google account, where we can read and reply to it like anyone else. Settings → "Rate Satzwerk" also lets you open the app's Play Store page yourself at any time. Google's processing is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Feedback by email

Settings → "Send feedback" opens a draft in your email app. It contains our address, a subject and – visible in the text – the Satzwerk version, the Android version and the manufacturer and model of your device. These details help track down bugs. You can delete them before sending. The app does not attach any training data. The email is only sent once you tap send in your email app. Satzwerk itself transfers nothing.

When you write to us – this way or directly – we process your email address, your name if it is given, and the content of your message in order to answer your request and fix bugs in the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, or Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for questions about a purchase). Our mailbox is hosted by WEB.DE (1&1 Mail & Media GmbH, Elgendorfer Straße 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany). We delete the correspondence once your request has been fully resolved and no statutory retention obligations prevent it.

## Data export and import

When you export or import a backup (JSON) or a CSV file, you choose the location or file yourself using Android's file picker. The app does not transfer anything. What happens to an exported file is up to you.

## Sharing

When you share a workout as text or as an image card (rank, records, level-up, achievements), the app creates the text or image on your device and hands it to the app you choose in Android's share dialog. Satzwerk itself sends nothing to us or to third parties. What the chosen app does with the content is governed by that app's privacy terms. The same applies when you recommend Satzwerk from the settings: only a short text with the link to the app on the Play Store is shared.

Workout routines are shared as a link. The routine – name, exercises, sets, reps, rest times and notes, but no weights – is contained in the link itself. No server stores it. Anyone who receives the link can read the routine. The page the link opens only reads the routine in your browser and does not transmit it. Visiting that page is covered by the section "This website". The app only adds a routine from a link once you tap "Import".

## Android backup

Satzwerk allows Android's built-in backup. If it is enabled in your device settings, Android can back up the app's data to your Google account and restore it on a new device. This backup is controlled by Android and Google. We have no access to it. You can turn it off in Android's settings under "Google" → "Backup".

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** for the rest timer and the current workout. The app only asks when you start a workout.
- **Vibration:** for the rest timer.
- **Internet and network state:** for Google Drive backups and purchases through Google Play.
- **Background execution** (wake lock, foreground service, start after reboot): so that automatic backups run reliably in the background.
- **Google Play billing:** for buying Satzwerk Pro.

Satzwerk does not request permissions for location, contacts, camera, microphone or body sensors.

## Data security

The data is stored in the app's private storage. Connections to Google Drive are encrypted via HTTPS, and the data exchange with the watch is encrypted. There is no database at the provider where users' data comes together.

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 15 to 21 and Art. 7(3) GDPR).
