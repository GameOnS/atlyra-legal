# Privacy policy for Atlyra

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This privacy policy covers the Atlyra app for Android phones and Wear OS watches as well as this website.

## In short

- Atlyra has no user account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics or crash reporting.
- Atlyra initially stores your training and nutrition data on your device. We as the provider do not see it – with one exception: if you use the assistant Atlyra AI and consent to it, the data needed for your question goes through our server to an AI provider (see "Atlyra AI").
- Data may also leave your device for optional backups to your Google Drive, the connection to your watch, Android backup, or when you export or share something. Purchases and emails use Google Play or your email app. When you search online for a product or look up a barcode in the nutrition diary, the request goes to the food database Open Food Facts (see "Nutrition diary").
- Health Connect is an optional connection: only once you set it up does Atlyra read health data from other apps – such as sleep, steps or weight – and store summaries of it on your device. It only goes to Atlyra AI if you share it separately (see "Health Connect").

## What the app stores

Atlyra initially stores your entries locally in the app's protected storage. The backups described below may optionally create copies:

- Workouts: exercises, sets, weights, reps, duration, distance, effort (RPE), notes and times
- Routines and custom exercises
- Body measurements if you enter them: body weight, body fat percentage and circumferences
- Heart rate and calories if you enter them manually for cardio exercises
- Points, ranks and levels that the app calculates from your workouts
- Settings such as units, weekly goal and rest time
- Result of the age check: on first start the app asks for your date of birth, because some features have a minimum age: the app cannot be used under the age of 13, the backup to Google Drive and Health Connect are open from 16, and Atlyra AI and the suggested calorie goal from 18. The date is checked on your device and then neither kept nor transmitted. Only the result is stored – for people under 18, the day from which these features are open. It is part of the settings, is not included in the Drive backup, and stays stored until you clear the app's data in the Android settings or uninstall the app.
- Coaching profile if you fill it in: training goal, experience, preferred session length, equipment, focus areas, exercises you would rather not do, and a note. It stays on the device and is included neither in the Drive backup nor in Android's backup.
- Nutrition diary if you use it: entries with food, amount, meal, day and nutrition values, your own foods, saved meals and recipes, and your nutrition goals. For calculating a calorie goal you can give your year of birth, height, sex and activity; this is not required.
- Values from Health Connect if you connect it: processed daily values of the last 90 days, such as sleep duration, steps, weight, nutrition, resting heart rate and heart rate variability (see "Health Connect"). They are not part of any backup.

Body measurements, heart rate, the nutrition diary, values from Health Connect and the note in the coaching profile (for example about an injury) can be health data. Apart from the age check, all entries are optional. The app does not read any sensors. It only accesses Health Connect if you connect it (see "Health Connect").

The only purpose is to provide the app's features: recording workouts, showing history and statistics, managing routines, recording and evaluating nutrition.

**Retention and deletion:** Local data is kept until you delete it. Settings → "Delete all training data" removes workouts, routines and body measurements from your phone, along with the conversation with Atlyra AI; custom exercises remain. The nutrition diary is deleted separately (see "Nutrition diary"), the values from Health Connect with "Disconnect" (see "Health Connect"). This does not delete Google Drive or Android backups or data on your watch. When you uninstall the app, Android removes local app data, but existing backups may remain. The steps for deleting Drive backups are below.

## Connection to your Wear OS watch

If you use the watch app, the phone and the watch exchange workouts, routines, exercise names, the current workout as well as points and ranks. The transfer uses the wearable interface of Google Play services: directly via Bluetooth or, if that is not available, via Google servers. According to Google, this transfer is end-to-end encrypted and only Atlyra on your own paired devices can read the data. Workouts stored on the watch stay there until they have been synced with the phone.

## Health Connect (optional)

Under Settings → "Health & data sources" you can connect Atlyra to Health Connect, the Android interface through which apps such as Samsung Health or MyFitnessPal make their data available. Without this connection Atlyra reads no data from other apps, and all other features work as before.

- **What Atlyra reads:** only the areas you select and allow in Health Connect – sleep (duration and sleep stages), steps, workouts from other apps (type, start, end, name), body weight, nutrition (energy, protein, carbohydrates, fat per entry), resting heart rate and heart rate variability. In addition, the name of the app a value comes from. Atlyra requests no other data types, in particular no medical records.
- **Why:** so Atlyra can look at your training together with sleep, everyday activity, weight and nutrition – in notes on the home screen, in the weight history and, if you share them separately, in answers from Atlyra AI.
- **Where the data is kept:** Atlyra stores processed values of the last 90 days (for example sleep duration per night, steps per day) in the app's protected storage on your device. They are excluded from Android backup and are part of neither the backup file nor the Google Drive backup. We as the provider never see them.
- **Sharing with Atlyra AI:** only if you additionally and explicitly share them. In that case summaries of this data, as far as a question needs them, travel the same way as all requests to Atlyra AI to our server and the AI provider (see "Atlyra AI"). The permission in Health Connect alone does not do that. If you take the sharing back, the app deletes a conversation that already contains such values.
- **Transferring workouts:** if you switch it on and allow it in Health Connect, Atlyra writes finished workouts to Health Connect as a session: name, start, end and type. Sets, weights and reps are not transferred. Other apps you give access to in Health Connect can read these sessions. If you change or delete a workout in Atlyra, the app changes or deletes the session in Health Connect as well.
- **Sending the nutrition diary:** if you switch it on and allow it in Health Connect, Atlyra writes the entries of your nutrition diary to Health Connect. For each entry that is the name of the food (with the brand, if given), the meal, a point in time and the calories and nutrition values of the amount you logged, as far as known: protein, carbohydrates, sugar, fat, saturated fat, fibre and sodium. Entries from the day you switch it on are sent, earlier ones are not. Other apps you give access to nutrition data in Health Connect can read these entries – including the names of the foods. If you change or delete an entry in Atlyra, the app changes or deletes it in Health Connect as well. Atlyra does not read back what it has sent itself.
- **No sharing with third parties:** Atlyra does not sell data from Health Connect, does not use it for advertising and passes it to no one except, as described, to Atlyra AI.
- **Legal basis:** your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give by connecting and by granting the permissions in Health Connect – for Atlyra AI additionally by tapping "Share".
- **Withdrawing and deleting:** you switch individual areas off on the "Health & data sources" page; Atlyra then deletes the values stored for them. When you switch off the transfer of workouts or of the nutrition diary, the app asks whether what has been sent so far should stay in Health Connect or be removed there. "Disconnect" stops reading and sending, gives the permissions back, deletes all cached values and ends the sharing with Atlyra AI. You can also revoke permissions in Health Connect itself at any time. What is stored in Health Connect and in the other apps is not affected by disconnecting – including workouts and diary entries Atlyra has sent there. If you do not want to keep them there, switch the transfer off with "Remove there" first, or delete them in Health Connect.
- **Limits:** values from watches, scales and nutrition apps are estimates and self-reported entries. Atlyra draws no diagnoses from them and is no substitute for seeing a doctor.

## Backup to your Google Drive (optional)

If you explicitly consent to connecting Google Drive in the settings, you sign in with Google through Google Play services and allow Atlyra to access a hidden app folder. You cannot view individual backup files in the regular Drive interface; Atlyra can read them for backup and restore. The app has no access to your other files in Google Drive.

- **What is transferred:** a compressed copy of all training data including body measurements and manually entered heart rate, and – if you use it – of the nutrition diary (entries, your own foods, saved meals and recipes, goals), plus the number of workouts as extra information on the backup file. Atlyra does not additionally encrypt this copy; transfer to Google Drive uses HTTPS. The ten newest backups are kept; the app deletes older ones.
- **When:** when you tap "Back up now" and – with Atlyra Pro – automatically every day and shortly after each workout.
- **Stored on the device:** the email address of the connected Google account (for display), time and checksum of the last backup.
- **Who receives the data:** The backups are stored in your own Google account. We have no access to them. Google's processing is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). For users in Europe the provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Data may also be transferred to the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
- **Legal basis:** your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give by tapping "Consent and connect". You can withdraw it at any time with "Disconnect": Atlyra stops further backups and removes the locally stored connection. Backups already uploaded remain until you delete them in Google Drive.
- **Deleting backups:** In Google Drive in your browser, go to Settings → Manage apps → Atlyra → Options → "Delete hidden app data".

## Nutrition diary (optional)

In the nutrition diary you record what you eat and, if you like, set goals for calories and nutrients. Whether you use it is up to you; training works without it. Like your training data, the diary is stored on your device. We do not see it.

- **Food database in the app:** The search for ordinary foods uses an extract of the German food composition database (Bundeslebensmittelschlüssel) that is installed with the app. Nothing is transmitted.
- **Product search and barcode (Open Food Facts):** When you search online for a packaged product or look up a barcode, the app queries the open food database Open Food Facts (Association Open Food Facts, 21 rue des Iles, 94100 Saint-Maur des Fossés, France). Transmitted are your search term or the barcode, the language of the search, and the app's name and version with our contact address – the service requires that from apps. As with any request on the internet, your IP address is processed. Your diary, your body measurements and your goals are not transmitted; there is no account. Before the first search the app points this out, and without your confirmation it sends nothing. A product you log is stored with its nutrition values in your own list so that it is available offline afterwards. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: the transmission is necessary to carry out the search you start. Processing by Open Food Facts is governed by their [privacy policy](https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy).
- **Barcode scanner:** The scanner is provided by Google Play services. They show the camera image and return only the number that was read to Atlyra; Atlyra itself gets neither access to the camera nor an image. Processing by Google is governed by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can type the number instead.
- **Goals and goal check:** The app estimates a calorie goal on the device from the details you give. The goal check works out – also on the device – from four weeks of diary and your body weight whether the goal fits what you burn. Both are estimates and not dietary advice.
- **Backups:** The diary is included in the Google Drive backup, in the exported backup and in Android's backup if you use them (see there).
- **Deleting:** In the nutrition diary, the menu → "Delete all nutrition data" removes the diary, your own foods, saved meals and recipes, goals and the details for the goal calculation from this device. "Delete all training data" in the settings leaves the nutrition diary in place.
- **Health Connect:** If you switch it on, Atlyra sends the diary's entries to Health Connect, where other apps can read them with your permission. The other way round, you can have the daily values of another nutrition app shown from Health Connect instead of your own diary (see "Health Connect").
- **Atlyra AI:** The assistant only sees the nutrition diary if you share it with it separately (see "Atlyra AI").

## Atlyra AI (optional)

Atlyra AI is an assistant inside the app. It answers questions about your training and proposes changes to your routines. If you share the nutrition diary with it, it also answers questions about that and proposes entries, meals or a nutrition goal. If you share data from Health Connect with it, it takes sleep, everyday activity, weight and nutrition into account in its answers. Unlike the rest of the app, it does not work on your device alone: to answer, the app sends data to a server.

Atlyra AI only becomes active once you explicitly consent when you first open it. Without consent nothing is transmitted, and every other feature of the app stays available as before. Atlyra AI is open only to adults aged 18 and over – this is why the app asks for your date of birth on first start – and is currently only available in the European Economic Area and Switzerland.

- **What is transmitted:** your messages to the assistant, the conversation so far and the training data needed for the question at hand. This can include routines, exercise names, notes, workouts with sets, weights and reps, and figures computed from them. This includes your coaching profile as far as you have filled it in (goal, experience, preferred session length, equipment, focus areas, exercises you would rather not do, and your note); you can switch this sharing off separately under "Privacy & data". Body weight and body measurements are only transmitted if you allow that separately. The same applies to the nutrition diary: only after you share it separately are daily totals and averages, your nutrition goal, on request the entries of a day with the names of the foods, and the names and ingredients of your saved meals transmitted, as far as a question needs them. The trend of your body weight for the goal check is only included if you have also shared body measurements. Data from Health Connect, too, is only included after you share it separately, and then only as summaries of the connected areas, as far as a question needs them – such as sleep duration per night, steps per day, weight trend, calories and nutrients per day, resting heart rate and heart rate variability as daily values. A photo of a meal is only included if you choose it yourself and have consented to that separately beforehand. The app scales it down and saves it anew, so details from the file such as location and camera are dropped; it is transmitted with the one request it belongs to. After that neither the app nor our server keeps the image – the conversation only notes that there was a photo. At Anthropic the same retention applies as for the other inputs. A picture often shows more than the food: make sure no people, documents or screens are in it. If you withdraw one of these permissions, the app deletes a conversation that already contains such details – otherwise they would keep travelling with the history. The app never transmits your entire data set. In addition there is technical information: a random identifier of your installation, the app's language and units, the app version, a proof of authenticity from Google Play and – if you have Atlyra Pro – the purchase identifier from Google Play. As with any internet connection, your IP address is processed; from it the server derives the country and region to check whether Atlyra AI may be offered there.
- **Who receives the data:** The request goes to a server operated by us at Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). It checks the request and passes it on to Anthropic (Anthropic Ireland, Limited, 6th Floor, South Bank House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, D04 TR29, Ireland), whose language model Claude generates the answer. Both process the data on our behalf. To verify the authenticity of the app and a Pro purchase, the server queries Google; for users in Europe the provider is Google Ireland Limited. Data is also transferred to the USA. Cloudflare, Inc. and Google LLC are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; with Anthropic the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission apply. They are part of Anthropic's [Data Processing Addendum](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/data-processing-addendum); we will send you a copy on request.
- **What is stored:** Our server stores no conversations – with one exception that you trigger yourself: answers you report (see "Reporting an answer"). It stores usage counters, linked to a non-reversible checksum of the installation or purchase identifier, and deletes them 40 days after your last request. To limit abuse, it also keeps a checksum of your IP address (for IPv6, of the network it belongs to) for at most two hours. For each request our server logs only figures: tier, duration, size, cost and an error code – no conversation content and no identifiers. Cloudflare keeps these logs, together with technical details of the call, for up to seven days and processes your IP address to deliver the request and to protect its network. According to Anthropic, it deletes inputs and outputs within 30 days and does not use them to train its models. Anthropic keeps content longer – up to two years – only if its automated abuse detection flags it as a violation of its usage policy, or if a law requires it. The conversation itself is stored only on your device, is excluded from Android backup and is deleted after 30 days without use.
- **Changes to your data:** Atlyra AI saves nothing on its own. Proposals appear as a preview and are only applied once you confirm them.
- **Reporting an answer:** With "Report answer" you can send us an answer from the assistant that is wrong, dangerous or offensive. What is transmitted is the text of that answer, the reason you choose, your note, the language and the app version – not the conversation and no identifier. If the answer mentions training, nutrition, health or body data, that is included; the dialog says so before you send it. We keep the report for 30 days on our server at Cloudflare and use it only to recognise unsuitable answers and to improve the assistant. Because it carries no identifier, we cannot link it to you afterwards. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by sending it (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR).
- **Legal basis:** your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time in Atlyra AI under "Privacy & data". Withdrawing deletes the conversation on your device and stops further transmissions. The checks against abuse (counters, proof of authenticity, country) are based on our legitimate interest in secure operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- **Limits:** Answers from an AI can be wrong. Atlyra AI does not give medical advice and is no substitute for seeing a doctor.

## Atlyra Pro and purchases

Purchases of Atlyra Pro are handled by Google Play. You only enter your payment details with Google; we never receive them. The app only receives purchase information from Google Play (product, status and a purchase identifier) to unlock Pro, and remembers locally whether Pro is active. On start, the app also checks once, locally, whether training data already exists so that early users keep their existing features. Nothing is transferred for this.

For billing, refunds and tax purposes, Google provides us with order information in the Google Play Console, such as order number, product, price, date and country. We only use it to process your purchase (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and to meet statutory retention obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR), which can be up to ten years. Google's processing is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The purchase is subject to the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/).

## Rating on Google Play

After a good workout, the app may ask Google Play to show you a rating window – at most twice. Google Play decides whether and when it appears. The app does not learn whether you rate or what you write. You submit your rating directly to Google. It appears in the Play Store under your Google account, where we can read and reply to it like anyone else. Settings → "Rate Atlyra" also lets you open the app's Play Store page yourself at any time. Google's processing is governed by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Feedback by email

Settings → "Send feedback" opens a draft in your email app. It contains our address, a subject and – visible in the text – the Atlyra version, the Android version and the manufacturer and model of your device. These details help track down bugs. You can delete them before sending. The app does not attach any training data. The email is only sent once you tap send in your email app. Atlyra itself transfers nothing.

When you write to us – this way or directly – we process your email address, your name if it is given, and the content of your message in order to answer your request and fix bugs in the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, or Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for questions about a purchase). Our mailbox is hosted by WEB.DE (1&1 Mail & Media GmbH, Elgendorfer Straße 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany). We delete the correspondence once your request has been fully resolved and no statutory retention obligations prevent it.

## Data export and import

When you export or import a backup (JSON) or a CSV file, you choose the location or file yourself using Android's file picker. The app does not transfer anything. The backup also contains the nutrition diary if you use it. What happens to an exported file is up to you.

## Sharing

When you share a workout as text or as an image card (rank, records, level-up, achievements), the app creates the text or image on your device and hands it to the app you choose in Android's share dialog. Atlyra itself sends nothing to us or to third parties. What the chosen app does with the content is governed by that app's privacy terms. The same applies when you recommend Atlyra from the settings: only a short text with the link to the app on the Play Store is shared.

Workout routines are shared as a link. The routine – name, exercises, sets, reps, rest times and notes, but no weights – is contained in the link itself. No server stores it. Anyone who receives the link can read the routine. The page the link opens only reads the routine in your browser and does not transmit it. Visiting that page is covered by the section "This website". The app only adds a routine from a link once you tap "Import".

## Android backup

Atlyra allows Android's built-in backup. If it is enabled in your device settings, Android can back up the app's data – including the nutrition diary – to your Google account and restore it on a new device. This backup is controlled by Android and Google. We have no access to it. You can turn it off in Android's settings under "Google" → "Backup". The values read from Health Connect and the settings for it are excluded from this backup.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** for the rest timer and the current workout. The app only asks when you start a workout.
- **Vibration:** for the rest timer.
- **Alarms and keeping the watch awake (watch app):** so the watch can signal the end of a rest with a vibration on time, even while its screen is off. Nothing is transferred for this.
- **Internet and network state:** for Google Drive backups, purchases through Google Play, Atlyra AI and the product search at Open Food Facts.
- **Background execution** (wake lock, foreground service, start after reboot): so that automatic backups run reliably in the background.
- **Google Play billing:** for buying Atlyra Pro.
- **Health Connect:** reading sleep, steps, workouts, weight, nutrition, resting heart rate and heart rate variability, and writing workouts and entries of the nutrition diary – each only once you switch the feature on and allow it in Health Connect.

Atlyra does not request permissions for location, contacts, camera, microphone or body sensors. The barcode scanner in the nutrition diary comes from Google Play services and needs no camera permission from Atlyra. You choose a photo for Atlyra AI in Android's picker; the app only sees the one image you choose and needs no access to your photos for it.

## Data security

The data is stored in the app's private storage. Connections to Google Drive, to the Atlyra AI server and to Open Food Facts are encrypted via HTTPS, and the data exchange with the watch is encrypted. There is no database at the provider where users' training data comes together; for Atlyra AI our server stores only the usage counters described there.
